DPGA Practice Console

A practice X32 Compact set up like the DPGASunday scene. Nothing here touches the real desk.

  • Use headphones. The stream is stereo; the house is mono.
  • Listen switches what you hear: the house, the stream, a wedge, an in-ear, or the selected channel (PFL).
  • Tap SEL on any strip to see its sends and where its signal stops.
  • Drills break one thing for you to find and fix.
  • Stage 4 opens EQ, compressor and gate on every channel, with harder drills (including feedback).
  • iPhone: turn the ringer switch on, or you'll hear nothing.

The band and voices are synthesized for now.
