DPGA Audio Troubleshooting

Updated 25 Nov 2026 · X32 Compact · S16 on AES50 B · Patch list v6.0
No soundNothing coming out of the house speakers
First: are the channel meters moving? Yes = check subgroups, Main LR, then everything after the console. No = before the console or the scene.
  1. Main LR fader up and not muted. No mute group button lit.
  2. DCA faders up and unmuted.
  3. Subgroups up and unmuted: vocal subgroup (Bus 13/14), instrument subgroup (Bus 11/12) and drum subgroup (Bus 9/10). Main LR is fed from the buses, so a muted subgroup is silent in the house. A muted subgroup silences every channel on it.
  4. Last DPGASunday scene loaded. Reloading resets mutes and faders to the saved starting point, as long as nothing was muted when the scene was saved.
  5. S16 connected on AES50 B with steady link lights. Reseat the cable between songs. Don't move it to port A.
  6. DriveRack powered, not muted, meters moving.
  7. Both amps on: Crown (subs) and Gemini (mains). No protect or stuck clip lights.
  8. Was everything powered in order? If not: amps off, check S16 + DriveRack, then amps on again.

The amps, S16 and DriveRack are in the equipment room, not at FOH. Signal path: X32 Main LR › S16 outputs › DriveRack › amps › speakers.

No soundMics and instruments silent, but the laptop and aux inputs still work
First: check the routing is on REC, not PLAY. In PLAY the channels listen to the card instead of the stage, so every mic and instrument goes quiet. The aux inputs (laptop, USB) aren't affected, which is the giveaway.
  1. Press ROUTING on the X32.
  2. On the routing screen, turn the rightmost knob to select REC.
  3. Push the knob in to confirm.

Reloading the scene won't fix this. Routing is scene-safed, so PLAY/REC stays wherever it was last left. PLAY is only for virtual soundchecks.

No soundOnly the subs (or only the tops) are missing
  • No subs: check the Crown Macro-Tech power and that it isn't in protect.
  • No tops: check the Gemini XGA-3000 power and that it isn't in protect.
  • Amp hot or shutting down? Check the equipment room AC is on (it gets switched off on generator).
No soundVideo, promo or playback has no sound
  • Videos / promos: AUX 1 (Visuals Laptop) unmuted and fader up. Check the laptop's volume and its cable.
  • Computer playback: AUX 5/6 (Card IN, over the USB cable).
  • USB drive playback: AUX 7/8 (USB L/R).
  • Ask the media team for a heads-up before any video plays.

AUX 3/4 (Stream Return) is for Zoom only and stays muted on Sundays, or the stream echoes.

MicsOne mic has no sound, but its meter is moving
  1. Channel unmuted, fader up.
  2. Its DCA (3, 4, 5 or 8) up and unmuted.
  3. No mute group lit. BGVs and the lead are often muted on purpose at the podium. Unmute the lead first, then the BGVs.
  4. Its subgroup up and unmuted: Bus 13/14 for vocals (podium mic too), the instrument subgroup (Bus 11/12) for the band. Vocals also need their send to Bus 13/14 on.
MicsOne mic has no sound and no meter
  1. Wireless: receiver on, mic switched on, battery good, RF meter showing signal.
  2. Corded: cable seated at the mic and the stage box; phantom on if it's a condenser.
  3. Instrument: DI connected and the instrument actually sending (keys are a stereo pair).
  4. Still nothing: hand them a spare corded mic. Don't re-patch during service.
MicsWireless mic cutting in and out
  • Battery meter low: swap batteries between songs. Fresh ones every Sunday.
  • Drops when the singer moves: clear the antennas, keep line of sight to the receivers, watch the RF meter.
  • Battery and RF fine: hand them a corded mic; check the wireless after service.
  • Went quiet during a speaking part: it was muted on purpose for feedback. Unmute before the next song.
Always: note the time and which mic. The pattern finds the cause.
FeedbackSquealing while someone speaks at the podium
First: mute the BGVs (and the lead if needed).
  1. Lower the podium mic's monitor send.
  2. Ask the speaker to stay about a hand-width from the mic and pointed at their mouth.
  3. Unmute lead, then BGVs, before the next song.

Never boost EQ to fix feedback. Pull the level down first.

FeedbackSquealing during singing, or from the headset
First: pull down the fader of the mic that's ringing.
  • Lower that mic's Bus 1 (wedge) send; keep wedges pointed away from the mic.
  • Headset (ch18): lower it a few dB and its monitor send; mic near the mouth, not the chest.
  • A mic nobody is using is ringing? Mute it until it's needed.
DistortionHarsh, crunchy or clipping sound
  • One channel: meter in red = gain too high. Lower it toward −18 dB.
  • Snare / drums hitting red: lower the drum gain; bring the drum DCA down if the room is too loud.
  • Band distorts after song one: they played softer at setup. Lower gains now; ask for full service level at setup next time.
  • Everything: Main LR in red. Lower Main LR or DCAs; check the amp clip lights.
NoiseHum, buzz, pops or clicks
  • Hum on one instrument: try the DI's ground lift; check the cable.
  • Hum on everything: usually power (generator, fans). Note the time and what was just switched on.
  • Pops or clicks on all channels: the AES50 link to the S16. Note the time; check the AES50 B cable is fully seated (between songs).
FXNo vocal delay, or the delay sounds wrong
First: the vocal delay is FX4 (Stereo Delay). It's fed by Bus 7 (Delay2) and comes back on the FX 4L/4R returns into the vocal subgroup. A mute anywhere along that path kills it.

No delay at all

  1. DCA 7 (Delay2) unmuted and up. It's muted in the scene.
  2. FX 4L/4R returns unmuted, faders up.
  3. Bus 7 (Delay2) unmuted, fader up.
  4. The lead and BGV channels' sends to Bus 7 are up. They're post-fader, so a muted or pulled-down vocal sends no delay either.

Delay sounds wrong

  • Out of time: tap the tempo on button #4.
  • Too much, or messy: lower DCA 7, or mute it for that song.
  • Echo on someone's speech: mute DCA 7 while they talk.
MonitorsSingers can't hear themselves
  • Raise their sends to Bus 1. Lead sits about 3–5 dB above the BGVs.
  • Both wedges silent: Bus 1 up and unmuted, wedges powered, daisy-chain cable between the two JBLs connected.
  • One wedge silent: check the link cable between the two wedges.
  • Musicians' in-ears: see the next card.

MonitorsMusician's in-ears silent, or missing something
First: is it one musician, or every in-ear at once? Every in-ear at once points to the AES50 B link to the S16.

One musician's whole mix is silent

  1. Their bus is unmuted with the fader up: Bus 3 Drums, 4 Keys, 5 Bass, 6 Extra.
  2. Bus 6 is muted in the scene: unmute it whenever someone is on it.
  3. DCA 6 (stage monitors) is up and no mute group is lit.
  4. The cable from their S16 output (3 drums, 4 keys, 5 bass, 6 extra) to their in-ear amp is plugged in at both ends.
  5. Their in-ear amp is powered with the volume up.

One instrument or voice missing from their mix

  • Raise that channel's send to their bus. Sends are pre-fader, so moving the house fader won't change their mix.
  • If they mix themselves on the app, check they haven't turned it down on their own phone.

Talkback

  • Can't hear the keys player or drummer at the desk: TB1–3 (ch 12–14) unmuted, and the M/C bus up.
  • Band can't hear you: the talkback mic is plugged into the X32's talkback socket. Talk A goes to the musicians (Buses 3–6), Talk B to the singers' wedges (Bus 1).
StreamNo audio on the livestream
First: solo MTX 1/2. Is the meter moving?
  • Yes: the problem is downstream. Ask the media team to check vMix first, and the ATEM last.
  • No: MTX 1/2 unmuted and fader up.
StreamStream sounds distorted
  1. MTX 1/2 not in the red. Lower it if it's clipping.
  2. Ask media to check the vMix input and master levels.
  3. Last: ATEM Mic 1 input set to line level (mic level caused distortion before). Don't change the ATEM master (it stays at −9 dB).
StreamVocals too loud or buried online, or speech too quiet
  • Press #8 (Sends on Fader: Stream) and adjust the Bus 13/14 send. This leaves the house alone. Press #8 again to turn it off, or the faders keep controlling the stream instead of the house.
  • Speech too quiet: run MTX 1/2 around −5 dB in P&W, raise it for speech.
  • Listen on headphones and a phone speaker.

The stream comes off the house buses, so house fader moves change the stream too.

StreamEcho, doubling, fan noise or phasey sound online
  • Echo / doubled audio: mute AUX 3/4 (Stream Return). It's only for Zoom meetings.
  • Fan noise or phasey sound: keep the crowd mic (DCA 8) down in P&W. Open it for the welcome and the responsive scripture reading.
  • Chat says audio dropped: get the time from media and log it.

After service, ask media to check stream health in YouTube Studio's Live Control Room.

PowerPower cut or switch to generator
First: switch off both amps so nothing pops when power comes back.
  1. Wait for the generator or mains to settle.
  2. Make sure the equipment room AC is on, or the amps can overheat.
  3. Power up in order: X32 › receivers + keys › S16 › DriveRack › both amps › stage monitors.
  4. Load the last DPGASunday scene; check the S16 link on AES50 B.
  5. Check the stream with media; log the time.

Lights flicker when the fans come on. If audio glitches at the same moment, write it down.

PowerX32 froze or restarted
  1. Bring the amps down or off if there's noise.
  2. Let it boot, then reload the last DPGASunday scene. Routing is scene-safed, so PLAY/REC won't change: check it's on REC.
  3. Recheck: vocal delay muted, BGVs about 6 dB under the lead.
  4. Note the time for the Weekly Log.
NormalThings that look wrong but are expected
  • Tom 1 (ch4) is muted: nothing is plugged into it.
  • BGV channels 19–22 are unnamed on purpose.
  • AUX 3/4 (Stream Return) is muted on Sundays.
  • The console reverb isn't working properly; the stereo delay is used instead (starts muted, tap on #4).
  • Virtual soundcheck gains don't carry over; set gains live on Sunday.
  • Both subs sit together on the left.

Golden rules
  1. Keep the service going. Pull the problem down first (mute or lower it), then fix it.
  2. Look before you touch. Check the channel meter: signal or no signal tells you where to look.
  3. One change at a time. Change one thing, listen, then decide.
  4. Don't touch routing. No changes to User In patching, AES50, the ATEM or vMix during service.
  5. Write down the time of every dropout, pop or squeal. Use the Log button.
  6. Ask early. Omar on keys? Use talkback. Otherwise message him after the song.
